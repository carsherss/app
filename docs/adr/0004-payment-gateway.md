# ADR 0004: Payment gateway

**Status: Proposed — not implemented in v0.1.1.**

## Context
CARSHERS may collect payments in India and will need server-side verification, webhooks, refunds and reconciliation.

## Decision
Razorpay is an initial candidate, subject to merchant onboarding, commercial review and confirmation of current fees and terms.

## Consequences
No payment SDK, credentials, endpoints or UI are included in v0.1.1.
